# Document View

**Document View** assembles your draft sections into one reading piece. Use it to hear the flow, polish the language and prepare a copy for someone else. It is **not** another saved draft: editing prose here changes the same sections you arrange in the [Workbench](workbench.md).

Only draft sections appear in the reading piece. [Parked thoughts](writing-a-piece.md) and private Storyboard notes remain in your project, not in this view's finished-piece export. A document title accompanies the assembled piece when you download it. For a structural change, return to Workbench rather than deleting across a section boundary in the reading editor.

At the top of Document View, **Copy whole piece** puts the assembled piece on your clipboard (formatted text where supported, with a plain-text version). **Download** offers **PDF**, **Word document (.docx)** and **Markdown (.md)**. You do not have to mark a piece “finished” or run a model before using these. **Ship / Send** is a manual path: **Copy for Notion** is for pasting yourself, and **Download for Obsidian** gives you Markdown to place in your vault. There is no direct account send or automatic sync.

The document-actions menu also has **Copy body as Markdown**, which omits the title for pasting just the prose. **Export Markdown** and Document View's **Download → Markdown (.md)** include the title as a finished-piece file. Document **Export JSON** is different: it preserves editable project data, including parked material, rather than making a reading copy. See [Export and delivery](export-and-delivery.md).
