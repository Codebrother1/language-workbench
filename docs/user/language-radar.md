# Language Radar

**Radar** is for examining how a phrase or language pattern is used, not for feeding the draft a trendy word. Open **Radar** in the header, enter a **Research topic**, then choose **Refresh live research** if the current provider supports web research. This is an explicit research request, separate from ordinary writing assistance. It is unavailable in Offline mode; saved items can still be read there, and the app does not invent fake trends or citations.

A radar item describes a term's meaning, mechanism, pattern, serious and ironic uses, an example structure and cautions. It includes source links and dates for discovery and the last research check. Those dates do **not** prove when people first used the term or that it is popular now. Newly researched items are for your review, not automatic approval. Set an item's status to **saved**, **maybe**, **dislike** or **never suggest**. No item inserts itself into your prose.

For one word already in your writing, select it and use the [Word/Phrase Lens](workbench.md#words-phrases-and-delivery) to explore or preview alternatives. Its Offline examples are curated, not live cultural research. For your own reusable phrases, use the [Personal Library](personal-library.md). See [Models and routing](models-and-routing.md) for provider choices and costs.
