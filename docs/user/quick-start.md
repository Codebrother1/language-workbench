# Quick Start

You can write with no brief, source, library, model choice or API key. A fresh setup without an OpenAI key uses the explicitly **Offline** provider. If you previously chose an OpenAI model and later removed its key, that choice stays saved but is unavailable until you change it or restore the key.

1. **Open Language Workbench.** Choose **Talk or type a thought**, or click the page and type. You can also paste your own words. The capture box **New thought** adds a Freeform card when you press Enter; Shift+Enter makes a line in that box. If you write directly on the page, you are editing the draft itself.
2. **Make a second part.** Use **Add draft section**, a boundary **+**, or **+ Add before / + Add after** on a selected card. Choose a section type, such as Point or Segue, or keep Freeform. Types name a job, not a form you must fill. Drag cards or use section options to change the order. [Writing a piece](writing-a-piece.md) has the structural basics.
3. **Set aside a thought if it does not fit yet.** **Park thought** moves that section below the draft into **Parked Thoughts**. It stays in this project and remains editable, but readers will not see it in Document View or finished-piece downloads. **Include in draft** asks where it should return.
4. **Read the piece.** Choose **Document View**. It assembles the draft sections in reader order. Edit a sentence there if you like; it is the same writing you saw in the Workbench, not a copy. Return to **Workbench** to move pieces around.
5. **Optionally ask a Lab question.** Select a passage inside one section, or focus a section. In the Inspector, give **Your direction** (for example, “Does this jump from the example too quickly?”), run a diagnosis, and answer its question in your own words before asking for creative proposals. For a selected word or short phrase, use **Lexical direction** in the Word/Phrase Lens to explore it or find replacements without that interview. Offline answers are limited fixtures, not a real reader or research service. See [Workbench](workbench.md) and [Models and routing](models-and-routing.md).
6. **Decide explicitly.** Inspect or edit a proposed version, then choose **Accept** or **Replace**, or **Reject** it. You can also save a [Take](takes.md). A diagnosis, preview, comparison or saved take does not replace your prose. If the underlying passage changed, ask again rather than expecting a stale proposal to land somewhere else.
7. **Share the draft you mean to share.** In **Document View**, choose **Copy whole piece** or **Download** → **PDF**, **Word document (.docx)** or **Markdown (.md)**. Check the assembled reading copy before sending it. See [Export and delivery](export-and-delivery.md).

That is a complete first session. [Writing Brief](writing-brief.md), [Sources](sources.md), [Style DNA](style-dna.md) and paid models can wait until you actually need them.
