# Revision checkpoint

A **Revision checkpoint** is one writer-set baseline for the **current draft**. Open **Revision** in the header and choose **Set revision checkpoint**, with an optional note such as “Before shortening the middle.” Later, **Review changes** shows section-level additions, removals, moves and edits since that point, with short before/now excerpts where useful. **Go to section** takes you back to a surviving part.

This is a low-noise comparison, not a comprehensive record of every keystroke or a separate editable draft. If you want the current draft to become the baseline, choose **Set current draft as new checkpoint** and confirm. That replaces the previous checkpoint. Setting or replacing one does **not** change your prose or save a [Take](takes.md).

To record what you still want to try, use [Revision Plan](revision-plan.md). To remember why this piece exists or where you stopped, use [Piece Memory](piece-memory.md).
